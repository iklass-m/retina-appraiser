AI appraisal (Gemini, free)

The app works without AI: every paper has an Appraise myself checklist. To also get automatic appraisals, add a free Google Gemini key once on each device.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Tap Create API key and copy it.
  3. Paste it below and tap Save and test.

Your key stays in this browser only. Google's free tier has a daily limit and Google may use free-tier requests to improve its products, so only send published papers, never patient details.

Claude (with a subscription)

Every paper has a Claude ↗ button. It opens your Claude version of this app at that paper, where Appraise uses your Claude subscription. Use Export / Import below to bring those appraisals back here.

Your library

Papers are saved on this device. To move them between your phone and PC (or to the Claude version), export a file and import it on the other device. Importing merges; nothing is overwritten unless the same paper is newer.
